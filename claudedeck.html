Privacy Policy for ClaudeDeck — Claude Folders & Chat Exporter

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

ClaudeDeck is a 100% local, free browser extension. It operates strictly inside your Chrome browser and interacts only with open https://claude.ai tabs when you explicitly click Sync Sidebar & Active Chat inside the Side Panel.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

  • Conversation Index, Folders & Active Chat Transcripts: Synced chat titles and URLs, custom folder names, pin flags, and active conversation text/code blocks are processed on-device and saved solely in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device so folders persist and exports work offline.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Transmission: ClaudeDeck does not use chrome.storage.sync and makes zero external network requests.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not transmit your Claude chats, prompts, folders, or exports to any external server or third party.
  • We do not collect account passwords, session cookies, analytics, telemetry, or browsing history across other websites.
  • We do not sell, rent, or transfer any user data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

ClaudeDeck does not use any third-party APIs, analytics SDKs, cookies, or external billing services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Your locally stored data stays strictly on your device until you click Clear Saved Workspace Data inside the Export tab or uninstall the extension.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised "Last updated" date prior to releasing a corresponding extension update.
