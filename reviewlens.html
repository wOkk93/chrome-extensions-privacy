Privacy Policy for ReviewLens — Amazon & Etsy Review Exporter

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

ReviewLens is a 100% local, free browser extension. It reads public customer review content from Amazon and Etsy product pages inside your browser only when you explicitly click Scan This Page or Auto-Collect in the Chrome Side Panel.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

  • Product & Review Data: Review text, star ratings, dates, variants, helpfulness counts, and public review image URLs extracted from the Amazon or Etsy listing you inspect are processed strictly on-device and cached solely in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) so your last scan stays available when reopening the panel.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Transmission: ReviewLens does not use chrome.storage.sync and sends zero data to external analytics, tracking, or license servers.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect browsing history across unrelated websites.
  • We do not collect account passwords, payment card details, or personal marketplace account credentials.
  • We do not use third-party analytics, tracking pixels, cookies, or advertising SDKs.
  • We do not operate any backend server, and we do not sell or transfer user data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

ReviewLens does not use any third-party APIs, analytics SDKs, cookies, or external billing services.

5. Data Retention and User Controls

Scanned reviews remain solely on your device until you replace them with a new scan, click Clear Saved Review Data inside the Export tab, or uninstall the extension.

6. Changes to This Policy

If our data practices change, we will update this document with a revised "Last updated" date prior to publishing a corresponding extension update.
