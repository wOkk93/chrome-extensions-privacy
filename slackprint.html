Privacy Policy for SlackPrint — Slack Channel Exporter & Printer

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

SlackPrint is a 100% local, free browser extension. It runs strictly inside your Chrome browser and reads messages from an open https://app.slack.com tab only when you explicitly click Capture Visible or Scroll Up History.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

  • Channel Transcripts: Sender display names, timestamps, message text, reaction pills, thread counts, and links captured from the channel you inspect are cached solely in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) so exports and contributor stats work locally.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Transmission: SlackPrint does not use chrome.storage.sync and makes zero external network requests.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not transmit Slack messages or exports to any external server.
  • We never request Slack OAuth tokens, passwords, or session cookies.
  • We do not run analytics, telemetry, cookies, or tracking scripts, and we never sell or transfer user data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

SlackPrint does not use any third-party APIs, analytics SDKs, cookies, or external billing services.

5. Data Control & Deletion

Click Clear Captured Messages inside the Export tab or uninstall the extension to erase all locally cached messages.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised "Last updated" date prior to releasing a corresponding extension update.
