Privacy Policy for MarkVault — X Bookmarks Organizer & Exporter

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

MarkVault is a 100% local, free browser extension. It reads bookmarked posts from X (x.com / twitter.com) inside your browser only when you explicitly click Capture Visible or Auto-Scroll Sync inside the Chrome Side Panel.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

  • Saved Bookmark Content & Folders: Post text, author display name and handle, timestamps, public media/link URLs, engagement counts, and your custom folder assignments are processed on-device and saved solely in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local).
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Transmission: MarkVault does not use chrome.storage.sync and makes zero external network requests.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect your X password, session cookies, or private direct messages.
  • We do not track browsing activity on unrelated websites.
  • We do not use analytics, cookies, telemetry, or advertising trackers.
  • We do not transmit, sell, or transfer your bookmarks or folders to any external server or third party.

4. Third-Party Services

MarkVault does not use any third-party APIs, analytics SDKs, cookies, or external billing services.

5. Data Retention and User Controls

Your bookmarks and folders stay solely on your device until you click Clear Local Library in the Export tab or uninstall the extension.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published here with an updated "Last updated" date prior to releasing corresponding extension updates.
