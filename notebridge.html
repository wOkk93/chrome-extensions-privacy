Privacy Policy for NoteBridge — NotebookLM Exporter & Study Pack

Last updated: November 21, 2026

1. Overview

NoteBridge is a 100% local, free browser extension. It executes strictly inside your Chrome browser and reads content from an open https://notebook.google.com or https://notebooklm.google.com tab only when you explicitly click Capture Notebook inside the Side Panel.

2. Data Processed and Stored Locally

  • Notebook Content & Study Exports: Source titles, studio notes, and Q&A chat turns from the open notebook are formatted on-device into Markdown, HTML study guides, JSON, and flashcards, and cached solely in Chrome's local storage (chrome.storage.local) on your device.
  • No Cloud Sync or Remote Transmission: NoteBridge does not use chrome.storage.sync and makes zero external network requests.

3. What We Do Not Collect

  • We do not upload your notebooks, notes, prompts, or study packs to any external server.
  • We do not collect Google account credentials, cookies, analytics, telemetry, or browsing history across unrelated websites.
  • We do not sell or transfer any user data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services

NoteBridge does not use any third-party APIs, analytics SDKs, cookies, or external billing services.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

Captured data remains solely on your device until you replace it with a new capture, click Clear Saved Notebook Data in the Export tab, or uninstall the extension.

6. Changes to This Policy

Updates to this policy will be published here with a revised "Last updated" date prior to releasing a corresponding extension update.
